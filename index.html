Associated players likelihood

The 0–100 likelihood score is a heuristic evidence index, not a calibrated probability. Weak candidates are exploratory. A user-confirmed relationship is a separate label and never changes the model score.

Every kill and death is considered. Shared-opponent interactions within 180 seconds include shared targets, shared threats and possible trade sequences. Continuous evidence separated by gaps of no more than 30 minutes is collapsed to limit double-counting one fight. Timing context considers both endpoints of events within 60 seconds.

Strength combines up to 10 shared-opponent windows (8 points each, reduced for popular opponents), up to 6 supporting dates (3 points each), volume-normalized timing excess (up to 10), timing context (up to 6), role-pattern diversity (up to 4), common-neighbour graph context (up to 5), UTC event-hour overlap (up to 4), distance habits (up to 1.5). Direct combat subtracts up to 40 points (8 per event). Sparse evidence shrinks the total before clamping to 0–100.

The timing baseline approximates accidental overlap from each player’s event volume within 30-minute buckets. It assumes uniform timing within those buckets; real fights violate that assumption. It is a comparison feature, not a statistical significance test. Correlated pattern features have small caps. UTC hour and distance habits are weak context only; each needs at least five relevant events. Weapon choice never contributes to association evidence. No actual online presence, location, timezone or sleep is inferred.

Inferred groups require at least two independent shared-opponent windows, strength ≥30 and no recorded direct combat. All three pairings must qualify for a trio. Groups are selected globally with no overlapping membership and a maximum of three people total. User-confirmed members are reserved first. Other candidates remain separate and may include combat opponents with explicit negative evidence.

Missing exports, common enemies, busy servers, third-party fights, farming and changing teams can mislead the model. There is no measured accuracy or probability calibration. Names linked to one profile ID are aliases; similar names on different IDs are not merged. Expand any candidate to inspect component evidence and example UTC events.
